Biography

I'm a first year Master's student in Computer Science at Colorado State University. I work as a Teaching Assistant for the Computer Science department and participate in research with the Computer Vision lab advised by Dr. Nate Blanchard. My primary research interests are in Computer Vision and Natural Language Processing.

Previously, I received my bachelor's degree from Michigan State University, where I was also a teaching and research assistant. I collaborated with the Heterogenous Learning and Reasoning Lab and presented at Mid-SURE 2024. I also worked with Whirlpool for 4 months during my senior design class developing a novel human machine interface for their ovens.
